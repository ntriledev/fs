## TL;DR

Three-phase escalation built around one idea: **the radar only speaks at pedestrian speed.**

1. **Pilot ($1.5–3k, 2 weeks):** BGC High Street 3D LED, 6–11 PM only — creative already done (`bgc-3d-led-creative.html` v3 → screen-record to MP4).
2. **Pedestrian network ($3–6k, 4 weeks):** Makati CBD evening peak + mall screens (12–8 PM) + one interactive bus shelter. QR goes here — 2 m viewing distance.

Skip EDSA Guadalupe for installs — 500k vehicles/day but nobody downloads from a moving car. Revisit only for brand-awareness later.

## 1. Why this shape

- **App mechanic = proximity radar.** It only makes sense where the viewer can
  *act now*: walking pace, phone in hand, 150 m of real people around them.
  BGC High Street at night IS the product demo.
- **Blindspot footfall data confirms the dayparts:** nightlife peak 8 PM–1 AM
  is exactly dating-app prime time; commuter peak 5–8 PM catches the Makati
  BPO crowd walking to dinner/transit.
- **Per-play pricing kills the risk.** $0.30–0.46/play, hour-level scheduling,
  verified plays + attribution. A wrong guess costs hundreds, not a ₱2M monthly
  commitment.
- **Manila > Lagos** (per existing research): cheaper, English-native,
  permissive dating culture, lighter regulation (ASC self-reg, media owner
  handles permits).
